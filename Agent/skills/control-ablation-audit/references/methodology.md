# Control and Ablation Methodology

ARR reviewer guidance states that a paper needs sufficient evidence for the
claim it makes and that a requested extra comparison must be justified as
necessary for validity. Meyes et al. (2019; arXiv `1901.08644`) provides ML
context for isolating component contribution through ablation. Hernan and
Robins (2020) provide broader causal-design context for contrasts and
alternative explanations.

The audit begins from a claim, not an ablation checklist. A component-causation
claim needs a contrast that changes that component while controlling material
alternatives. A whole-system performance claim can remain valid without
identifying which component caused the result.
